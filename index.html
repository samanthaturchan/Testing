About

I graduated with an Honours Bachelor of Journalism and managed social media on a national level at major news orgs. That newsroom training mixed with digital marketing experience lets me wear multiple hats at once to get the job done: strategy, content, paid media, podcast production, and the AI workflows now holding it all together.

Here's my take on 2026: anybody can generate content now. Far fewer people can tell you whether it's accurate, whether it sounds like you, or whether it's worth publishing at all. I use AI to move faster (research, first drafts, twenty ad variants instead of two, turning one podcast episode into a month of content) and I use fifteen years of editorial judgment to decide what actually ships.

I take pride in well-researched marketing initiatives, learning something new every day, creating efficiencies, and teamwork. People who know me also say I'm naturally curious and have a desire for continuous improvement... it's all true.
